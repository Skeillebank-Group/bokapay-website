How it works

Four steps, a few seconds each. Your customer needs nothing but their phone. In our tests, "Paid" shows on your phone about a second after your customer pays.

  1. Enter the amount

    Type what the customer owes in the BokaPay app.

  2. Show the QR code

    Your customer scans it with their phone camera, or you send them a payment link by WhatsApp or text.

  3. They pay

    With Pay by Bank in their own banking app, by card, or by a normal bank transfer to your account.

  4. You see "Paid"

    Automatically for Pay by Bank and card. For a bank transfer, you tap "Received" when it lands.

Money is paid out to your bank account on your payment account's normal payout schedule.

Your money, your account

BokaPay never holds your money. Payments go directly to your own payment account, in your business's name, and from there to your bank.

  • Pay by Bank and card payments are processed by Stripe. You set up your own Stripe account when you sign up, and Stripe verifies your business.
  • Bank transfers go straight to your bank account. Your customer sees your details, the amount and a unique reference, with copy buttons. No Stripe account needed.
  • Refunds go back to the customer the same way they paid.
  • Pay by Bank means your customer approves the payment in their own banking app. No card details to type or steal.
  • Changing your bank details takes 24 hours, and every owner gets an email, so nobody can quietly redirect your payments.

Questions

Do I need a card machine?

No. You use the BokaPay app on your phone, and your customer uses theirs.

Can I still take cash?

Of course. BokaPay doesn't replace cash; it gives your customers more ways to pay. Soon you'll also be able to record cash payments in BokaPay, so every payment for a job is in one place for you and your accountant.

Is BokaPay available now?

Not to everyone yet. BokaPay is built and tested, and we're looking for tradespeople in Northern Ireland to use it first in a pilot. Get in touch if you'd like to take part.

Does it work on Android and iPhone?

Yes. During the pilot you add BokaPay to your home screen from your phone's browser, where it works like an app, with notifications. Apps in Google Play and the App Store will follow.

When do I get my money?

Bank transfers go straight into your bank account. Pay by Bank and card payments land in your Stripe account and are paid out to your bank on your normal payout schedule.

Do I need a Stripe account?

Only for Pay by Bank and card. You can start with free bank transfers using just your bank details, and add Stripe later; we set it up for you during sign-up.

What if my customer's bank doesn't support Pay by Bank?

They can pay by card or by bank transfer instead, on the same page.

Can I give a refund?

Yes, full or partial. Pay by Bank and card refunds are made from the app. For a bank transfer, you send the money back from your bank and record it in the app.

Does BokaPay ever hold my money?

No. Payments go straight to your own bank or Stripe account.

Does BokaPay help with Making Tax Digital?

BokaPay keeps a digital record of every payment you take through it, which you or your accountant can use for your records. It doesn't send anything to HMRC itself; your accounting software or accountant still does that.

What do I need to sign up?

Your business details and bank account. For Pay by Bank and card, Stripe also verifies your business. Sole traders are welcome.
