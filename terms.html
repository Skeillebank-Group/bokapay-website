2. What BokaPay is

BokaPay is software that lets businesses ("you", the merchant) request payments from their customers, for example by showing a QR code or sending a payment link, and see when those payments are made.

BokaPay is a software service. We are not a bank or a payment institution, and we do not receive, hold or transfer your money.

3. How payments reach you

Bank transfer. If you offer it, BokaPay shows your customer your own bank details, the amount and a reference, and your customer pays your bank account directly. BokaPay doesn't initiate or see the transfer: you confirm in BokaPay when it has arrived, and BokaPay records what you confirm. You must only enter a bank account held in your business's or your own name.

Pay by Bank and card, processed by Stripe:

  • To take Pay by Bank and card payments you open your own payment account with Stripe through BokaPay. That account is in your business's name, and you accept Stripe's own terms (the Stripe Connected Account Agreement) directly with Stripe.
  • Your customers' payments go to your Stripe account and are paid out to your bank by Stripe on your payout schedule. They never pass through an account held by us.
  • Stripe verifies your business and may ask you for information. Stripe decides whether to accept your account and may restrict it under its terms.
  • Stripe charges its processing fees on your account. Its current rates are shown on our pricing.
