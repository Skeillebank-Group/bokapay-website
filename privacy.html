Privacy Notice

Pilot version · Last updated 10 October 2026

Who we are

BokaPay is operated by Skeillebank Ltd (company no. NI744153), 8 Manse Road, Drumquin, BT78 4SD. For anything about your personal data, email info@bokapay.co.uk.

Businesses that use BokaPay, and their staff

We are the controller of this data.

  • What we collect: name, email address, business name and details; the business's bank details if it offers bank transfers (shown to its customers so they can pay; changes are recorded and every owner is told); staff accounts, roles and invites; records of the payments the business requests and receives through BokaPay, and who confirmed bank transfers; which version of our terms the business accepted, when and by whom; and the notifications we send the owners, kept in the app's history for 90 days.
  • Security emails: when a business's bank details change, or an owner is added or removed, we email every owner, including one who was removed, so a change made from someone else's sign-in can't go unnoticed.
  • Why: to provide BokaPay under our terms (contract), to keep records that the law requires (legal obligation), and to keep the service secure and fix problems (legitimate interests).
  • How long: while the account is open, then payment records for 6 years for accounting and legal reasons. Other account data is deleted within 90 days of closing the account.

Customers who pay a business through BokaPay

When you pay a business through BokaPay, that business is the controller of your data and we process it on its behalf.

  • What we hold: the amount, what it was for, the payment method and status, and the date and time; your email address if you ask for a receipt, used only to send it and deleted after 90 days; and, for a bank transfer, that you tapped "I've paid" and when. You don't need an account to pay, and we never receive your card details or bank login.
  • What Stripe tells us: when you pay by card or Pay by Bank, Stripe sends us a message about the payment, which can include your name, email address, phone number and address, depending on what you gave Stripe. We use it only to record the payment, and delete it after 7 days (30 days if it couldn't be processed and needs checking).
  • If you pay by bank transfer, you pay the business directly from your own banking app; we don't see your bank details.
  • Pay by Bank and card payments are handled by Stripe, which processes your card or bank details as its own controller. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • We never read your bank account.

People who contact us

If you email us, for example to join the pilot, we use your message and contact details to reply, and delete them within 12 months if you don't become a customer.

Technical data

  • Connection data: IP addresses, to protect the payment pages from abuse while a request is handled. Our service monitoring masks the IP address. Changes to a business's settings are recorded in an audit log with the network they came from (the IP address with its last part removed) and the browser or app used, kept with the business's records.
  • Error records: what went wrong and where, with ids rather than names or emails, deleted after 90 days.
  • Devices for notifications: if an owner turns on notifications, the phone or browser's notification address, so we can send them. Removed when they sign out, and dropped if the device hasn't been used for 60 days.
  • On the device itself: the app keeps the businesses you belong to and recent takings (amounts, times and whether each was paid, never what a payment was for) so it can open without a signal, and cancellations made offline until they can be sent. Signing out deletes them. In a web browser, the sign-in lasts only while the browser session does.
  • We don't collect location data, and we don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

Who we share data with

  • Stripe, which processes payments and verifies businesses.
  • Microsoft (Azure, UK South region), which hosts our service and data, provides sign-in, sends receipt and security emails, and monitors the service.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging and Chrome's push service) and Apple, which deliver app and browser notifications.

We don't sell personal data or use it for advertising. Where a provider handles data outside the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or the UK's approved data transfer agreements.

If Skeillebank Ltd's BokaPay business is sold, merged or transferred, the personal data it holds would pass to the new owner, who would have to keep to this notice. We'd tell businesses beforehand.

How we keep data secure

  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored encrypted in Microsoft Azure data centres in the UK (UK South).
  • Keys and passwords are kept in a dedicated key vault, never in our code.
  • Each business can only ever see its own data; this is enforced in the database for every request.
  • Access to systems is limited to the people who need it, with two-step sign-in, and changes are recorded in an audit log.
  • We don't store card details or bank logins. Card and Pay by Bank details are handled by Stripe, which is certified to the highest payment card security standard (PCI DSS Level 1).
  • Our logs record ids and outcomes, not names, emails or message contents.
  • If a security incident affects your data, we'll tell you and, where required, the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies and no tracking. The payment pages set one strictly necessary security cookie that protects their forms, and nothing else.

Children

BokaPay accounts are for businesses and the people who work for them, not for children. We don't knowingly hold a child's data in an account; if we find we do, we'll delete it. Anyone can pay a business through BokaPay; that payment is the business's record.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, and ask us to correct or delete it, or to restrict or object to how we use it. You can also ask for the data you gave us in a format you can take to another service (data portability). If you paid a business through BokaPay, you can also ask that business directly. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters to businesses using BokaPay, we'll tell them by email and in the app before it takes effect.
