Refunds and Complaints

Pilot version · Last updated 3 October 2026

If you paid a business through BokaPay

  • Refunds are given by the business you paid. Your payment went to that business's own account, so contact them first about a refund, or about a problem with the goods or service.
  • When a business gives a refund in BokaPay, the money goes back the same way you paid: to your bank account for Pay by Bank, or to your card. It usually arrives within 5–10 working days, depending on your bank.
  • If you paid by card and can't resolve it with the business, your card provider may be able to help.
  • If you think a payment was taken by mistake, or you don't recognise it, contact your bank and tell us at info@bokapay.co.uk.

If you're a business using BokaPay

You can give full or partial refunds from the app, up to the amount paid. Refunds are made from your Stripe account. Stripe doesn't charge for a refund, but keeps the fee for the original payment.

Complaints about BokaPay

If you're unhappy with BokaPay itself, for example the app or this website, email info@bokapay.co.uk. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 5 working days and aim to resolve it within 15 working days. If we need longer, we'll tell you why.
